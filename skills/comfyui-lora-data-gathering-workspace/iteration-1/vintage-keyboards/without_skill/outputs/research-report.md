# Research Report: Vintage Mechanical Keyboards (Baseline - No Skill)

## Overview
Research findings for collecting vintage mechanical keyboard images for LoRA training.

## Potential Image Sources

- **Reddit r/MechanicalKeyboards** — Large enthusiast community, search "vintage" or "retro" for collection posts
- **Deskthority.net** — Dedicated vintage keyboard wiki and forum
- **eBay listings** — IBM Model M, Apple Extended Keyboard listings with photos
- **ClickyKeyboards.com** — Restoration specialist, product photos
- **Keychron retro collection** — Modern retro-styled boards

## Key Models to Include
- IBM Model M (beige, buckling spring)
- Apple Extended Keyboard II
- Commodore 64 keyboard
- AZIO retro collection
- Unicomp New Model M

## Visual Characteristics
- Beige/cream plastic cases
- Thick bezels and chunky proportions
- PBT keycaps with colorful legends
- Coiled cables
- Industrial 1980s-90s design language

## Training Notes
- Use SDXL at 1024px
- Need 20-30 varied images
- Mix of product shots, collection photos, and detail close-ups
