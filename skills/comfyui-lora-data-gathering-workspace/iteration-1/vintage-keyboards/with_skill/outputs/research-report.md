## Key Visual Qualities to Capture

- **Color**: Warm beige/cream cases, sometimes with grey accents. Keycaps in PBT cream with colorful legends or accent keys
- **Form factor**: Full-size or TKL, thick bezels, industrial proportions
- **Details**: Coiled cables, DIN connectors, prominent branding badges, thick keycap profiles (SA, DSA)
- **Context**: On desks, with CRT monitors, or clean product-shot style
- **Texture**: Matte plastic cases, textured keycaps, visible switch mechanisms in profile shots

## Recommendation

Start with r/MechanicalKeyboards (search "vintage collection" posts — collectors often post beautifully photographed sets). Supplement with Deskthority wiki pages for specific models and ClickyKeyboards product shots. Use eBay listings selectively for varied angles. Target 25-30 images with diverse models but consistent retro aesthetic.
