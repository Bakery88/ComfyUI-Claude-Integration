# Research Report: Yoshitaka Amano JRPG Watercolor Style

## Subject Analysis

**What the LoRA should learn**: Yoshitaka Amano's signature watercolor/ink illustration style as seen in classic Final Fantasy concept art. Key visual qualities:
- Ethereal watercolor washes with fine ink line work
- Elongated, graceful figure proportions
- Luminous color palettes (deep blues, purples, golds, crimsons)
- Flowing organic forms — hair, fabric, and armor blend together
- Blend of ukiyo-e, Art Nouveau, and Western fantasy traditions
- Loose brushwork around strong pencil strokes
- Charcoal smears and acrylic splatters as accents

**LoRA type**: Style LoRA (not subject-specific)
**Base model**: FLUX (1024px target)

## Ethical Notes

- Amano's art is copyrighted. Training a personal LoRA is common practice but redistribution of the LoRA or commercial use should be considered carefully.
- Internet Archive scans are community-uploaded — use for personal training only.
- Prefer official/gallery sources where possible for highest quality.

## Recommendation

Start with the Final Fantasy Wiki gallery (well-organized, categorized by game) and the Internet Archive art book scans (highest resolution). Supplement with Pinterest boards after filtering for quality. Target 25-30 images focusing on his watercolor/ink work, avoiding his more recent digital or 3D collaborative pieces which would dilute the style signal.
