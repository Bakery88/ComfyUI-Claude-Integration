# Research Report: Yoshitaka Amano JRPG Watercolor Style (Baseline - No Skill)

## Overview
Research findings on Yoshitaka Amano's watercolor/ink style for LoRA training. Based on general knowledge (web search was unavailable).

## Key Visual Characteristics
- Ethereal watercolor washes with ink line work
- Elongated, graceful figure proportions
- Rich color palettes with luminous highlights
- Flowing organic forms and dramatic compositions
- Blend of Eastern and Western art traditions

## Potential Image Sources

### Art Books
- **The Sky: The Art of Final Fantasy** — Comprehensive collection of FF concept art
- **Dawn: The Worlds of Final Fantasy** — Environmental and character art
- **Beyond the Fantasy** — Later FF works
- **Vampire Hunter D art books** — Non-FF Amano work, same style

### Websites
- amanosworld.com — Official portfolio
- Final Fantasy Wiki (finalfantasy.fandom.com) — Game art galleries
- Danbooru/Gelbooru — Tagged anime art archives

### Other
- CivitAI — Check for existing Amano-style LoRAs as reference
- Auction house listings (Christie's, Sotheby's) — High-res scans of original art
- ArtStation — Fan art inspired by Amano

## Training Challenges
- Amano's style varies significantly across decades
- Watercolor effects are difficult to capture in LoRA
- Need to distinguish Amano's personal style from generic "JRPG art"

## Legal Considerations
- Amano's art is copyrighted — training data use for personal LoRA is a gray area
- Fan art may be more accessible but less stylistically pure
