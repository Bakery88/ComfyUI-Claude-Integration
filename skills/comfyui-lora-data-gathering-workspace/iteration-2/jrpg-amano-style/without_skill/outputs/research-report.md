# Research Report: JRPG Watercolor Art Style (Baseline - No Skill)

## Overview
Gathering reference images for Yoshitaka Amano's watercolor JRPG art style from Final Fantasy for LoRA training.

## Potential Sources
- **Final Fantasy Wiki** — Concept art galleries for each game
- **ArtStation/DeviantArt** — Fan art and studies
- **Pinterest** — Amano art boards, varied resolution
- **Google Image Search** — "Yoshitaka Amano Final Fantasy art"
- **CivitAI** — Check existing Amano-style LoRAs for reference

## Key Visual Elements
- Flowing watercolor washes and ink lines
- Ethereal, dreamlike character designs
- Muted palette with gold and crimson accents
- Art nouveau decorative framing
- Elongated figure proportions

## Training Notes
- FLUX at 1024px
- 25-30 images
- Focus on finished illustrations
- Florence-2 or BLIP-2 for captioning
