# Research Report: Cyberpunk Anime Style (Baseline - No Skill)

## Overview
Research for collecting cyberpunk anime art in the style of Ghost in the Shell and Akira for LoRA training on SDXL.

## Potential Sources
- **Danbooru/Safebooru** — Tagged anime art archives, search cyberpunk + cityscape + neon tags
- **Pinterest** — Large Ghost in the Shell and Akira boards
- **DeviantArt** — Cyberpunk anime fan art
- **Halcyon Realms** — Anime art book reviews and production art documentation
- **CivitAI** — Check for existing cyberpunk anime LoRAs as reference

## Key Visual Elements
- Neon-lit megacity environments
- Cybernetic enhancements on characters
- Detailed mechanical designs (mecha, motorcycles, weapons)
- Dark atmospheric palette with vivid neon accents
- Hand-painted cel animation look
- Rain, reflections, fog, atmospheric effects

## Shows to Source From
- Akira (1988)
- Ghost in the Shell (1995)
- Bubblegum Crisis (1987)
- Psycho-Pass
- Blade Runner: Black Lotus

## Training Notes
- SDXL at 1024px
- 30+ images for style diversity
- Focus on background art and key animation frames
- WD14 tagger recommended for anime content
