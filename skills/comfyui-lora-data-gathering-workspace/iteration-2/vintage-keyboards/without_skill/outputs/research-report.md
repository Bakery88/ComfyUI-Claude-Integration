# Research Report: Vintage Mechanical Keyboards (Baseline - No Skill)

## Overview
Research for collecting images of vintage mechanical keyboards for LoRA training. Focus on the retro beige-case aesthetic with colorful keycaps.

## Potential Sources
- **r/MechanicalKeyboards** — Reddit community with frequent vintage posts
- **Deskthority Wiki** — Keyboard encyclopedia with photos
- **eBay listings** — Product photos of vintage keyboards for sale
- **Google Image Search** — "vintage mechanical keyboard" high-res
- **YouTube** — Keyboard review and restoration videos

## Key Visual Elements
- Beige/cream plastic cases
- Colorful keycap sets
- Chunky industrial design
- Coiled cables
- Period desk setups

## Training Notes
- FLUX at 1024px
- 20-30 images of various keyboards
- Product photography style
- Auto-caption with Florence-2 or BLIP-2
